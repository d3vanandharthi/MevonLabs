Craft has many forms.

Mevon Labs brings AzulejosGoa, Ceramic Decals and Ranjan Engravings together under one parent-company identity.

One family.
Three perspectives.

Hand-painted ceramic art, custom ceramic transfer printing and stone engraving solve different needs. Their connection is the way personal text, artwork and meaning guide the brief.

Each business maintains its own specialist approach. The group website makes it easier to discover the right team without pretending that every material uses the same process.

What the parent website does

Mevon Labs introduces the relationship between the brands, explains their services and provides a direct route to the relevant specialist. It offers planning guides so your first inquiry can be more informed.

What stays with each brand

Design development, technical recommendations, pricing, production and fulfillment are agreed with the business responsible for the work. A combined production contract or shared timeline is not automatically included.

Our name, in context

The Mevon Labs name identifies the parent company. This site describes craft and customization businesses, not unconfirmed scientific research, technology services or certifications.
