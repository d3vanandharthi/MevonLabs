Ceramic vs Stone Nameplates: Choosing for Your Home

Reviewed October 11, 2026 · Confirm project-specific details directly with the brand

A nameplate identifies an entrance and gives it character. Within the Mevon Labs family, ceramic and engraved stone offer different visual approaches. The right choice begins with the intended look and actual mounting location.

Choose the character

Hand-painted ceramic is a route for borders, color and illustrated motifs. Stone engraving emphasizes the material, lettering and contrast. View real examples on the specialist sites before choosing a direction.

Prepare the brief

  • Measure the available width and height.
  • Write the exact family name and house number.
  • Share a photograph of the entrance.
  • Describe whether the placement is covered or exposed.
  • Ask about mounting, finish and readable lettering.

Confirm the full order

Check the approved design, spelling and numbers. Ask for the current quote, production schedule, shipping or installation charges and the relevant terms. Do not assume both materials have the same workflow.
