Release information dated October 11, 2026. The operator must review this notice and confirm actual hosting practices before publication.

WhatsApp drafts

The inquiry form prepares a message in your browser. It does not save details to a website database or send email. Your name, location and project description are included in a WhatsApp link when you continue. WhatsApp may receive this information when the link opens; the message reaches the business when you send it there.

Do not include identity documents, payment details or sensitive personal information.

No tracking in this release

The supplied site includes no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, website-set cookies or local-storage inquiry database. The hosting provider may keep request logs, IP addresses and other access data. The operator must confirm the provider and its logging and retention settings.

External services

WhatsApp and the specialist websites have their own policies. Email links open your email application. Once you contact a business, ask it about handling, retention and deletion of the information you provide.

Before launch

Confirm the legal entity, dedicated privacy contact and hosting practices. Update this notice if server-side inquiries, analytics, accounts, uploads or payments are added. This release notice is not a certified legal-compliance policy.
