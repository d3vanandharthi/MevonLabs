Stone Engraving in Goa: Preparing Your Brief

Reviewed October 11, 2026 · Confirm project-specific details directly with the brand

Ranjan Engravings offers personalized stonework in Goa. A clear brief helps the specialist understand the significance of the design and the practical requirements of the site.

For a memorial

  • Write names, dates and the complete inscription exactly.
  • Include language and symbol references.
  • Provide the best original photograph if relevant.
  • Share site details and installation restrictions.
  • Ask the business to confirm any required date.

For a sign or nameplate

Include text or logo, dimensions, viewing distance and mounting location. Discuss the stone, contrast and appropriate engraving technique.

Agree installation separately

Confirm who measures, transports, prepares the support and installs the piece. Include access requirements, charges and scheduling explicitly in the quote.

Proofread the approved design

Check spelling, dates, symbols, line breaks and image treatment. Ask how changes after production begins are handled.
