Hand-painted ceramic art

AzulejosGoa specializes in Portuguese and Indo-Goan ceramic art. Personalized text, ornament and palette are part of the design conversation. Discuss the actual dimensions and placement, then review the proposed layout before production.

Explore AzulejosGoa

Printed ceramic transfers

CeramicDecals.in offers custom artwork printing on waterslide paper, including overglaze and underglaze options. These are technical choices connected to the substrate, glazing and firing workflow.

Request product-specific instructions and test representative pieces. Food-contact suitability depends on the finished object as well as the transfer, material and firing process.

Explore ceramic transfers

Engraved & carved stone

Ranjan Engravings lists traditional carving and precision engraving techniques. The right method depends on the stone, design detail, lettering contrast and scale. For installed work, discuss site access and mounting separately.

Explore stonework

Ask before you approve

  • Which material and method suit this exact design?
  • What proof or sample will I review?
  • What is included in the quote?
  • What are the current timing and fulfillment arrangements?
  • What care or technical instructions should I follow?
