Custom Ceramic Decals: Planning Your Order

Reviewed October 11, 2026 · Confirm project-specific details directly with the brand

A good ceramic decal order begins with the artwork and the object it will decorate. CeramicDecals.in handles custom waterslide transfers for makers and businesses. Share the actual workflow so the specialist can advise on the appropriate option.

Prepare the artwork

  • Supply the best available file and final printed dimensions.
  • State the quantity of every design.
  • Identify logos, photos, fine lines and small text.
  • Ask about sheet arrangement and achievable color results.

Explain the process

Share the substrate, glazed or unglazed state, intended use and firing workflow. Obtain product-specific instructions from the supplier. This guide is not a firing schedule.

Test before scaling

Food-contact suitability depends on the transfer, substrate, firing process and completed piece. Request supporting information and test representative finished samples before commercial production.

Repeat orders

Agree the approved artwork version, sampling approach, quantity, invoicing and shipping. Mention expected repeat volumes so consistency can be discussed.
